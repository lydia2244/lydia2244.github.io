Original Iced Capp

Ingredients

Iced Capp Base Mix: Sugar, water, brewed coffee extract, caramel colour, and natural and artificial flavours.

Cream Component (18% Dairy Cream): Milk, cream, and stabilizers for texture (such as carrageenan, disodium phosphate, mono- and diglycerides, and sodium citrate).

Matche Creamy Chill

Ingredients

Matcha Green Tea Powder / Mix: Gives the drink its earthy green tea flavor and vibrant green color..

Frozen Cream Base / Neutral Creamy Base: The signature iced/frozen blended slush foundation.

Milk / Dairy: Used to create the creamy base.

Blended Creamy/Frappe Base: A frozen base mixture that provides the smooth, icy, milkshake-like texture.

Sweetener / Syrup: Adds the signature sweetness to balance out the natural bitterness of the matcha.

Whipped Cream Topping: A layer of whipped cream on top, sometimes finished with a light dusting of matcha powder.
